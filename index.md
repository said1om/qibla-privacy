## English

**Last updated:** 26 November 2026

Qibla & Dhikr does not collect any personal data and does not require an account.

**Location:** Your location is used on-device to compute the Qibla direction and prayer times. The app may use Apple's geocoding service to show a city name. We do not store your location on our servers or share it with anyone.

**Local storage:** Your settings (calculation method, language, theme, tasbih counter, last reading position) stay on your device only.

**Notifications:** Local notifications are used for adhan and adhkar reminders, scheduled on your device.

**Widget and Apple Watch:** Settings and prayer times are shared only between the app, its widget and your Apple Watch.

**Update check:** The app contacts Apple's iTunes Lookup service to detect newer versions, without sending personal data.

**External links:** The app may open external links governed by their own policies.

**Children:** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes:** This policy may be updated; the date above will change accordingly.
